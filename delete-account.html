Request account and data deletion

You can delete your Last-Minute Jobs account and its associated data at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us from the address associated with your Last-Minute Jobs account
  2. Let us know you'd like your account and data deleted
  3. We'll confirm your identity and process the request
This works whether or not you still have the app installed.

What gets deleted

Requesting deletion removes your profile, account credentials, saved preferences, and any photos, videos, or voice notes you've uploaded.

Some information tied to completed jobs — payment records, transaction history, and messages exchanged as part of a job — may be retained for a limited period afterward where needed for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes, consistent with our Privacy Policy. This data is not used for any other purpose once your account is deleted.
