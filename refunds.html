Cancellations & refunds

This is a plain-language summary. The full rules are in section 8 of the Terms of Service, which prevail.

Before anyone accepts

A Poster can cancel a job at no charge.

After a Tasker accepts

  • Either person can cancel free of charge within 15 minutes of the acceptance.
  • After 15 minutes, a Poster who cancels pays a fixed cancellation fee of 15 in the job's currency (or the job price, if lower). The app shows the fee before you confirm.
  • A Tasker can cancel up to 2 times per week without penalty. Further cancellations lower their rating by 0.3 points each. No money fee is charged to the Tasker.
  • If the Tasker does not arrive within a reasonable time after the agreed start, the Poster may cancel and is refunded the amount held.

Fees

The Poster pays the job price plus a service fee of 12% of the price (minimum 2.50 in the job's currency). The Tasker pays a 5% commission on the job price. Minimum job price: €12, US$15, £12 or CHF 15. The full amount is only held on the Poster's card once a Tasker accepts, and the fee is shown before you confirm.

Jobs that are never finished

If a Tasker accepts a job but never starts it, we send both people a warning and then close the job at no cost to either side: after 12 hours for ASAP and same-day jobs, or 7 days for planned jobs. The held amount is released in full. A job that was started but not finished for 48 hours is sent to us for review.

When the work is done

The payment is held when a job is accepted and released when the Poster confirms. The Poster has the confirmation window shown in the app (currently 24 hours) to confirm or report a problem. We send the Poster a reminder after 20 hours. If nothing is done, the payment is released to the Tasker automatically at 24 hours. The Poster may ask for one extra photo or video as proof, once per job; this does not pause the 24-hour window. Disputes are reviewed using the messages, photos and timestamps in the app, and we may refund, release or split the payment as we judge fair. This does not limit your legal rights, including the right to go to court or, in the EU, your consumer rights.

Payouts to Taskers

Taskers are paid through Stripe. Their share goes to their Stripe balance when the job is completed, then to their bank account. A new account's first payout can take about 7 days; after that payouts are weekly. The app shows the expected arrival date for each job.

Card payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. A held amount that is refunded goes back to the original card; your bank can take a few days to show it.
