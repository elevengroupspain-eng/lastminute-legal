Cancellations & refunds

This is a plain-language summary. The full rules are in section 8 of the Terms of Service, which prevail.

Before anyone accepts

A Poster can cancel a job at no charge.

After a Tasker accepts

  • Either person can cancel free of charge within 15 minutes of the acceptance.
  • After 15 minutes, a Poster who cancels pays a fixed cancellation fee of 15 in the job's currency (or the job price, if lower). The app shows the fee before you confirm.
  • After 15 minutes, a Tasker who cancels has their rating lowered by 0.3 points. No money fee is charged to the Tasker.
  • If the Tasker does not arrive within a reasonable time after the agreed start, the Poster may cancel and is refunded the amount held.

When the work is done

The payment is held when a job is accepted and released when the Poster confirms. The Poster has the confirmation window shown in the app (currently 24 hours) to confirm or report a problem. If nothing is done, it is released to the Tasker automatically. Disputes are reviewed using the messages, photos and timestamps in the app, and we may refund, release or split the payment as we judge fair. This does not limit your legal rights, including the right to go to court or, in the EU, your consumer rights.

Card payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. A held amount that is refunded goes back to the original card; your bank can take a few days to show it.
