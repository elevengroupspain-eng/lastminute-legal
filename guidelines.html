Community rules & safety

For everyone

  • Be honest: use your real details and describe jobs accurately.
  • Be respectful. Harassment, threats and discrimination lead to a ban.
  • Keep payment in the app. Paying outside it removes our ability to help in a dispute.
  • Do not share personal contact details in a job listing. Use the in-app chat.
  • Follow the law where the job is done. Jobs listed in our Terms as prohibited are removed.

Safety tips

  • Meet in public where possible, and tell someone you trust where you are going.
  • Check the profile, ratings and reviews. A "verified" badge only means an ID photo was approved by us. It is not a background check.
  • Never hand over cash, cards or valuables, and never share codes or passwords.
  • Trust your instincts. If something feels wrong, leave and report it in the app.
  • In an emergency, call 911 (United States) or 112 (Spain and the EU) first.

If you are a Poster

  • Describe the task, location, time and who supplies materials.
  • Check the Tasker's qualifications yourself for regulated work. For child care, meet the Tasker first and never leave a child with someone you do not trust.
  • Agree extra work or a higher price in the app before it starts.
  • Confirm the job or report a problem within the confirmation window (currently 24 hours); otherwise the payment is released.

If you are a Tasker

  • You decide whether to accept a job and how to do it. You are an independent person, responsible for your own taxes, insurance and licences.
  • Only accept jobs you are legally allowed and able to do.
  • Do not buy materials expecting repayment unless the Poster approved the item and amount in the app.
  • Tell the Poster before continuing if the work is bigger than described.

Reporting a problem

Use the report button on a job, message or profile, or write to elevengroupspain@gmail.com with the details. We review reports and may remove content or suspend accounts. See section 23 of the Terms of Service.
