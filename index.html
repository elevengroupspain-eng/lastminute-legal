Need something done today? Someone nearby can do it.

Last-Minute Jobs is a mobile app that connects people who need a task done at short notice with people nearby who are free to do it. It is a marketplace: we introduce the two sides and handle the payment, and the tasker does the job.

See how it works

Find jobs on the map

Taskers see open jobs around them as pins on a map. Filters narrow the view by category, such as errands, pet care, moving help, cleaning or plumbing. Tap a card to see the price, the distance and the details.

Or browse the list

Each job shows the price, how far away it is, how long until it expires, and the poster's rating. One tap on Accept and the job is yours.

Post a job in under a minute

Pick a quick template or describe what you need, choose a category and set the price and the place. A payment method is required before posting, so a tasker knows the job is real.

Build trust with a profile

Taskers choose their skills, describe their qualifications and experience, and can verify their identity with an ID photo. Qualifications are shown to the people who hire them and are marked as provided by the tasker.

How a job goes

  1. Post. Describe the task, set the price and pin the location.
  2. Accept. Someone nearby accepts. You can see their rating and the jobs they have completed, and you can message them in the app.
  3. Do the job. The tasker goes to the place and does the task.
  4. Payment is released. When the job is marked complete, the money is released to the tasker. Both sides then rate each other.

How payment works

Payments are processed by Stripe. When a tasker accepts a job, the agreed amount is held on the poster's card. It is not paid out until the job is done. If the tasker does not arrive within a reasonable time, the poster can cancel and the held amount is refunded.

Our fees: the person who posts a job pays the job price plus a service fee of 12% (minimum 2.50 in the job's currency). The tasker pays a 5% commission on the job price, so they keep 95%. The fee is shown in the app before a job is posted or accepted. There is no charge for browsing or posting.

Refunds and disputes

If something goes wrong, either side can open a dispute in the app. We review the messages, photos and timestamps from both people and may refund, release or split the payment as we judge fair. Cancellation rules, including late cancellations and no-shows, are shown in the app before you confirm. Full details are in our Terms of Service.
