Last-Minute Jobs is made and run by VAELEVEN, S.L., a company based in Estepona, Costa del Sol, Spain.

Why we built it

Small jobs come up at the worst time: a delivery to collect, a flat to clean before guests arrive, furniture to move, a pet to look after. Finding someone reliable usually means group chats and waiting.

At the same time, many people nearby have a free hour and would like to earn. We built Last-Minute Jobs to connect the two quickly and fairly, starting in our own area.

How it keeps people safe

  • Payment is held securely and only released when the job is done. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store card numbers.
  • Taskers can verify their identity, and profiles show ratings and completed jobs.
  • Posters can approve who comes: Taskers ask to do a job and the poster chooses. Child care jobs always need approval.
  • Both sides can report a problem from inside the app.
