How long we keep data

Summary for our Privacy Policy. Controller: VAELEVEN, S.L., NIF B75754176, Estepona (Málaga), Spain.

Retention periods

  • Account details (name, email, phone, profile): while your account is active, then deleted or anonymised after you delete the account, except what we must keep below.
  • Photos, videos and voice notes attached to a job: deleted automatically 30 days after the job is completed or cancelled, unless needed for an active dispute or investigation.
  • Job records, messages and payment history: while the account is active and afterwards for as long as needed for tax, accounting, security, fraud-prevention and dispute purposes (in Spain, accounting records are generally kept for six years).
  • ID photo and verification status: while the account is active, and deleted when you delete the account or if verification is withdrawn, unless needed for an open investigation.
  • Support tickets and reports: as long as needed to handle them and show how we acted, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Crash and diagnostic logs: kept for a limited period for troubleshooting.
  • Waitlist contact details: until we have told you about the launch or you ask us to remove them.
  • AI API keys: only a hash is stored, and it is removed when you revoke the key or delete the account (usage totals may be kept without personal details).

Deleting your data

Use the delete-account page or write to elevengroupspain@gmail.com. We act within one month.
