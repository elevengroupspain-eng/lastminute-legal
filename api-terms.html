AI assistant & API terms

These terms add to our Terms of Service for people who use an API key with software or an AI assistant.

What a key can do

A key may post a job, list your jobs, check the status of your jobs and confirm completion. Everything else (account, payments, accepting jobs, admin) is refused. Keys are created in the app, under Settings, by verified users, and shown once.

Your responsibility

  • You are responsible for everything done with your key, including prices, payments, cancellations and any breach of the Terms, as if you had done it yourself.
  • Payment is made from the card saved on your account. The key owner, not the AI, is the payer.
  • Do not use a key to post prohibited jobs, to spam, or to avoid our rules or limits.
  • Keep keys secret and revoke them in the app if they may have leaked.
  • Check what your assistant posts. Jobs it posts are labelled as posted by an AI assistant.

Limits

Each key has limits on requests per hour and on the number and total value of jobs per day. We may change limits, suspend or revoke a key at any time to protect users or the Service. The API is provided as is and may change.
